# Privacy Policy for 读书派

Effective date: June 10, 2026

读书派 is designed as a local text reading and narration app. We do not collect, track, sell, or share personal data from this app.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store imported text files, pasted text, reading progress, app settings, and prompt templates locally on your device. If you use the Share Extension, shared text is transferred between the extension and the main app through the app's local App Group container.

## Wi-Fi Transfer

The Wi-Fi transfer feature works only on your local network. Text files are sent directly from your computer to your device. We do not receive or store those files on any server.

## Third-Party Services

The app can optionally open a Perplexity search in your browser when you choose that action. In that case, the text you send is handled by the third-party service and is subject to that service's privacy policy.
